## Companion activities

The activity below is a new portfolio exercise. It illustrates a practical learning task; it is not presented as a historical student submission or as proof of learner results.

### Exercise: Plan a campaign for an adult training seminar

**Audience:** a beginner digital marketer working with a training provider.

**Learning goal:** connect a business objective, audience, message, lead form, and measurement plan.

**Task**

1. Choose one city and one adult learner segment.
2. Write a seminar objective and distinguish registrations from attendance.
3. Draft two advertising messages: one about the skill to learn and one about the practical application.
4. Describe the information genuinely needed on the registration form.
5. Write the confirmation message and next step for a registrant.
6. Create a simple results table for spend, valid leads, attendance, and cost per valid lead.
7. Explain what you would change if registrations were high but attendance was low.

**Submit:** one campaign brief, two copy variations, a form outline, and a measurement table.

**Assessment guide**

| Criterion | What a good submission shows |
| --- | --- |
| Objective | A clear action, time period, and success measure |
| Audience | A specific learner need rather than everyone in a city |
| Message | Clear benefits and accurate course claims |
| Form | Necessary fields, consistent location details, and a clear next step |
| Measurement | Correct calculations and separation of leads from attendance |
| Reflection | A sensible explanation of what to investigate next |
